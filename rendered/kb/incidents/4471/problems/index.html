#4471 is closed, but one problem in it is not. Dates that move by one day across time zones were never part of the parser fix. Reports like these still belong to #3, not to #4471.

The problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, so day and month swap: a 7 April reminder goes out on 4 July. This is what #4471 says it is. Issue #1.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and reads what people type day first. People do what the hint says, and the booking swaps. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. The cause table on #4471 already shows this group as "no ticket of its own"; fixing the parser does not touch it. Issue #2.

one-day move: dates shift by one day across time zones. No swap: Lisbon gets Tuesday, Toronto gets Monday, for the same meeting. The desk noted these reports as a one-day move and then filed them under parser by tenant. They are listed in desk-notes.json. Issue #3.

How the reports were counted

  • The export comes in overlapping pages, so some reports appear twice. Each id counts once.
  • causeOf() in causes.ts places a report by its tenant's locale and form, not by intake's tag. Intake tags from the reporter's browser; it disagrees with the tenant in both directions.
  • Counted the desk's way, as exported: reports in desk-notes.json → oneDayMove stay counted under parser, where their tenant files them, and get their own row and issue as well.

Why it merged as one

Decision 0001 merges on subject line. All three problems produce the same subjects ("Wrong day on the invite", "Date is wrong"), so subject matching cannot tell them apart. Its consequences only foresee the opposite case, one bug split across subjects. A new decision record should replace it; 0001 stays as accepted.
